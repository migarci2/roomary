01 / The idea

Continuity is a feeling.
Details make it believable.

A chair facing the wrong way. A window that moves between chapters. A doorway that could never connect. Small spatial mistakes pull readers out of a world.

Roomary gives every location a persistent visual memory, so you can write forward without losing the room behind you.
